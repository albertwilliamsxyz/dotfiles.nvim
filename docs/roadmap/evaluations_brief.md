# Plugin Evaluation Scope (Brief)

This document outlines the scope of the upcoming **Evaluation Report**.

## 1. AI & "The Neural Link"
*   **Avante.nvim vs CodeCompanion**: Feature parity, "Cursor-like" experience vs "Agentic" capabilities. Deletion viability?
*   **Persistence**: Strategies for saving AI sessions.

## 2. Visuals & Media
*   **Media**: `render-markdown`, `image.nvim`, `nui.nvim` (Image previews in Oil/Popups).
*   **UI Polish**: `twilight`, `indent-blankline`, `rainbow-delimiters`, `colorizer`, `dressing.nvim`.
*   **Pro Config**: Deep dive into `nvim-notify` and `nui.nvim` customization.

## 3. The "Mini.nvim" Consolidation
*   **Replacement Candidate**: Can `mini.nvim` replace `nvim-surround`, `ultimate-autopair`, `treesitter-textobjects`?
*   **Pairing**: `mini.pairs` vs `nvim-autopairs` (Logic for "not generating a pair").

## 4. Navigation & Editing
*   **Motion**: `flash.nvim` vs `leap`.
*   **Granular Motion**: `spider` (sub-word motion).
*   **Manipulation**: `dial.nvim` (Inc/Dec), `yanky.nvim` (Ring), `substitute.nvim`.

## 5. Version Control Stack
*   **Comparison**: `fugitive` vs `neogit` vs `git-worktree` vs `gitsigns` vs `octo`.
*   **Diffing**: `diffview.nvim` vs Fugitive.

## 6. Code Intelligence
*   **Structure**: `aerial.nvim` vs `outline.nvim`.
*   **Context**: `treesitter-context` (Check status) vs `lsp_lines.nvim`.
*   **Snippets**: `blink.cmp` (Native) vs `luasnip`.

## 7. Workflow & Testing
*   **Task Running**: `overseer.nvim` evaluation.
*   **Testing**: `neotest` setup.
*   **Debugging**: DAP capabilities deep dive.
*   **Undo**: `undotree`.
